Child Safety Standards

Effective 2026-09-03 · Last updated 2026-10-08 · Applies to VOZ Mobile on Android & iOS

VOZ Mobile (developer: Tuong Lam Photos) is an unofficial, independently-developed client app for the voz.vn discussion forum. This page states our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), consistent with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Zero tolerance for CSAE, no exceptions

Our standard: CSAE is prohibited

VOZ Mobile prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. Using the app to view, post, share, request, solicit, or promote child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or to groom, sexually exploit, or traffic minors, is strictly forbidden. The developer will not build, enable, or keep any feature that facilitates such conduct, and will act on any CSAE concern specific to the app.

What VOZ Mobile is — and isn't

VOZ Mobile does not host, store, or operate any servers, accounts, or content. It is a reading and posting client: every thread, post, image, and message a user sees is fetched live from voz.vn and displayed as-is. VOZ Mobile does not cache media on any server it controls — cached data lives only on the user's own device.

Content moderation, account discipline, and legal compliance for material hosted on the forum are the responsibility of voz.vn, the platform that stores and serves that content.

In-app reporting

Every post in VOZ Mobile carries a "Báo cáo bài viết" (Report post) action. A report — with a required reason — is submitted immediately to voz.vn's own moderation queue through voz.vn's native report endpoint, the same system used by voz.vn's official website and moderators. This applies to any post, including suspected CSAE material.

Reports are not queued or reviewed by the app developer — they go directly to voz.vn's moderation team, who have the authority and access to remove content and act on accounts.

Escalation and law enforcement

Because VOZ Mobile stores no user content, the app developer has no CSAE material to independently investigate or transmit. Any report of suspected child sexual abuse material encountered through the app should be filed, in order of directness:

  • Through the in-app Report post action described above (fastest — reaches voz.vn's moderators directly), or
  • To the platform operator, voz.vn, directly, or
  • To the relevant national authority (e.g. NCMEC's CyberTipline for reports with a US nexus, or the appropriate authority in the reporter's country).

The developer of VOZ Mobile will cooperate with law enforcement and with Google regarding any report specific to the app itself, to the extent of information available to an app that stores no server-side user data.
